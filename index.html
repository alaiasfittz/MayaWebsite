About Maya

Maya Ogunjobi is an NFL cheerleader, content creator, and marketer based in Florida. Her content brings together sports, wellness, lifestyle, and the brand moments that naturally connect to those worlds.

With three years of digital marketing and audience-insight experience at Electronic Arts, Maya understands both the creative and strategic sides of content. She is now bringing that perspective into her creator journey while sharing her love for dance, healthy routines, brands, culture, and learning along the way.
